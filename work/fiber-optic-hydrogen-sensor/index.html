01 — Research / Photonics

Fiber-Optic Hydrogen Sensor

MS Thesis

Role

Researcher, MS thesis

Category

Research / Photonics

Year

2026

Status

In progress

Overview

WO₃ + Pd bilayer coated on chemically etched single-mode optical fiber (~9 µm core region), with intensity-based evanescent-wave sensing at 1310 nm. WO₃ is deposited by RF and Pd by DC magnetron sputtering, using a ring-coating approach for uniform coverage around the fiber. The focus is low-temperature crystalline WO₃ without high-temperature annealing.

What I did

  1. 01Chemically etched single-mode fiber down to the ~9 µm core region so the guided light reaches the coating
  2. 02WO₃ sensing layer by RF magnetron sputtering, Pd catalyst layer by DC magnetron sputtering
  3. 03Ring-coating approach for uniform coverage around the fiber
  4. 04Intensity-based evanescent-wave readout at 1310 nm
  5. 05Working toward crystalline WO₃ at low temperature, without high-temperature annealing

Tools & methods

  • RF / DC magnetron sputtering
  • Fiber etching
  • 1310 nm optical power
  • XRD
  • Microscopy
